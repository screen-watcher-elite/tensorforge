Linear Algebra Theory & Shortcuts

🎓 FY Engineering Curriculum Reference

  • Matrix as Coordinate Basis: The columns of $A = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$ define where standard basis vectors $\hat{i} = [1, 0]^T$ and $\hat{j} = [0, 1]^T$ land.
  • Determinant ($\det A$): Represents the signed area scaling factor of any 2D region. If $\det < 0$, space is flipped (orientation reversed). If $\det = 0$, space collapses into 1D or a point.
  • Eigenvalues ($\lambda$): Solutions to the characteristic equation $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$. Invariant directions where $A\vec{v} = \lambda \vec{v}$ (vectors only scale, never rotate).
  • Non-Commutative: In general, $AB \neq BA$ because the order of consecutive geometric transformations matters!
